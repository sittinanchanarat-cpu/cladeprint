# Published label tables

Supplementary data tables of the three population studies whose lineage labels are used (read by `labels/build_labels.py`). Cite the original articles when reusing them:

- `rhodes2017/`: Rhodes J et al. (2017) Genetics 207:327–346 (Tables S1–S7).
- `desjardins2017/`: Desjardins CA et al. (2017) Genome Res 27:1207–1219 (Supplemental Table S1).
- `ashton2019/`: Ashton PM et al. (2019) Nat Commun 10:2035 (Supplementary Data, MOESM5).
